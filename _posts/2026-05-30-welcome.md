每一個開始，都是一種承諾。

*Every beginning is a kind of promise.*

---

這個個人網站存在已經有一段時間，但它一直只是一個連結清單——連到我散落在各地的文字、研究、社群帳號。今天，它開始有了自己的聲音。

*This personal website has existed for a while, but it was always just a list of links — pointing to my words, research, and social accounts scattered across the internet. Today, it begins to have its own voice.*

---

## 為什麼要寫？Why Write?

從藥學、臨床藥學、國際衛生，到醫療科技評估（HTA），我的學術路徑讓我習慣了用「證據」說話。但證據之外，還有很多值得思考、值得記錄的事——關於科技與社會的交界、關於知識管理的實踐、關於這個讓我好奇不斷的世界。

*From pharmacy and clinical pharmacy, to global health and Health Technology Assessment, my academic path has trained me to speak with evidence. But beyond evidence, there is much worth thinking about and recording — the intersection of technology and society, the practice of knowledge management, and a world that never stops making me curious.*

這個網站，將是我思考的落腳處。

*This website will be where my thinking lands.*

---

## 這裡會有什麼？What to Expect?

- **研究觀察**：HTA、藥物政策、實證醫學的評論與分析
- **AI 與科技**：AI 輔助工作流程的實驗與反思
- **人文思考**：歷史、藝術、社會的不定期凝視
- **知識管理**：MySecondBrain 與個人知識系統的實踐

*Research observations, AI & technology experiments, humanities reflections, and knowledge management practices.*
