# 當指定捐款變成一場單向通知——我與某NGO的一日經歷

今天發生了一件事，讓我對於台灣NGO的捐款治理，有了全新的感受。

我是一位長期定期定額捐款人，每月固定捐助一個專門服務移工孩童的兒童安置計畫，至今已持續整整一年。這筆捐款是我的**指定捐款**，用途明確，對象具體。

## 第一封信：單方面通知轉用途

今天中午，我收到這個基金會寄來的一封信。信中告知，該計畫因「階段性任務調整」，將於今年八月底結束服務，並說明：我原本指定給該計畫的定期定額捐款，**七月起將自動轉入基金會的「兒童醫療協助方案」**。

信中沒有解釋：

- 所謂「階段性任務調整」究竟是什麼意思？
- 原本服務的移工孩童，後續將由誰接手照顧？
- 為何選擇「兒童醫療協助方案」作為承接項目？
- 這個新方案的服務對象與原計畫是否有任何關聯？

更關鍵的是：**這是通知，不是徵詢同意。**

指定捐款的本質，在於捐款人將信任與善意，交付給一個明確的用途。在未獲捐款人同意的情況下，逕行變更指定用途，在法律與倫理上，都是值得質疑的做法。

## 我的回信：提出疑問

收到信後，我隨即回覆，提出上述幾個問題，語氣平和，只是希望得到合理的說明。

## 一小時後：捐款被暫停

沒有任何人致電、沒有任何人回信、沒有任何人嘗試溝通。

約一小時後，我收到了金流平台的系統通知：**我的定期定額捐款，已被暫停。**

操作方，是基金會。

從頭到尾，沒有一通電話，沒有一封解釋，沒有任何人與我接觸。就這樣，一筆已執行一年、原設定為長期的指定捐款，在我提出疑問的當天，被單方面終止。

## 我想說的話

我無法確定這樣的處理方式是否違法，但我可以確定的是：

這樣的處理態度，**不像一個以信任為基礎的慈善組織應有的樣子。**

捐款人不是客戶，但也不是提款機。每一筆指定捐款的背後，都有一個人選擇相信這個組織、相信這個用途。當一個NGO面對捐款人的善意質疑，選擇的回應方式是沉默加上單方面停款——這傳達的訊息很清楚：

**你的信任，不重要。你的疑問，不歡迎。**

台灣的公益勸募環境需要更高的透明度與問責標準。我選擇將這件事公開，不是為了對立，而是希望每一位有心捐款的人，在交出善意之前，都能得到應有的尊重與說明。

## 來和我交流

如果你也有類似的經歷，或對於台灣NGO的捐款治理有看法，歡迎留言交流。

# When Designated Donations Become One-Way Notifications: My Experience with a Taiwan NGO

Something happened today that has given me a whole new perspective on donation governance in Taiwanese NGOs.

I am a long-term donor who makes fixed monthly contributions to a children's placement program that specifically serves migrant workers' children. I have been donating consistently for exactly one year. This donation is a **designated donation**, with a clear purpose and specific target.

## The First Letter: Unilateral Notice of Purpose Change

This afternoon, I received a letter from the foundation. It informed me that the program, due to "phase-based task adjustments," would be ending its services by the end of August. The letter also stated that my monthly designated donation would **automatically be redirected to the foundation's "Child Medical Assistance Program" starting in July**.

The letter provided no explanation for:

- What exactly "phase-based task adjustments" means
- Who will take over care for the migrant children previously served
- Why the "Child Medical Assistance Program" was chosen as the successor program
- Whether the new program's target population has any connection to the original one

Most importantly: **This was a notification, not a request for consent.**

The essence of a designated donation lies in the donor's entrusting their goodwill to a specific purpose. Unilaterally changing that purpose without donor consent is ethically and legally questionable.

## My Reply: Raising Questions

After receiving the letter, I immediately replied, posing the questions above in a measured tone, simply hoping to receive a reasonable explanation.

## One Hour Later: My Donation Was Suspended

No one called. No one replied. No one attempted to communicate.

About an hour later, I received a system notification from the payment platform: **my monthly donation had been suspended.**

The action was taken by the foundation.

From start to finish: no phone call, no explanation, no contact of any kind. Just like that, a donation I had been making for a year, originally set up as a long-term commitment to a specific purpose, was unilaterally terminated on the very day I asked for clarification.

## What I Want to Say

I cannot determine whether this approach violates any law, but I can say with certainty:

**This is not how a charitable organization built on trust should behave.**

Donors are not customers, but neither are they ATM machines. Behind every designated donation is a person who has chosen to trust this organization and this purpose. When an NGO responds to a donor's well-intentioned questions with silence and unilateral action to suspend funding—the message is crystal clear:

**Your trust doesn't matter. Your questions are unwelcome.**

Taiwan's charitable fundraising environment needs higher standards of transparency and accountability. I'm making this public not to create conflict, but to ensure that every person considering a donation can receive the respect and explanation they deserve before giving their goodwill.

If you've had a similar experience, or have thoughts about donation governance in Taiwanese NGOs, I welcome your comments.
