關於我

About

生命的每一個轉折，都在累積成現在的我。從藥學、臨床藥學、國際衛生，到醫療科技評估（Health Technology Assessment, HTA），每一步都拓寬了我看世界的視角。探索從未止步於專業——藝術、人文、歷史、社會，乃至科技與社會的深層互動，同樣是我理解這個世界不可或缺的座標。在這段短暫的人生中，我希望帶著好奇，持續深探，留下值得回顧的足跡。

Every chapter of my journey has shaped who I am today — from pharmacy and clinical pharmacy, to global health, and Health Technology Assessment (HTA). My curiosity extends beyond the professional: art, humanities, history, and society — and the profound interplay of science, technology, and society — are equally essential lenses through which I understand the world. In this brief life, I hope to keep exploring with wonder, and leave behind something worth looking back on.
